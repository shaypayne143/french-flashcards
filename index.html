How to read “sounds like”
CAPITALS = say that syllable a little stronger (French stresses the last one)
(n) = nasal vowel: hum it through your nose, don't fully say the n
zh = the “s” in measure · uh = the “a” in about
ew = say “ee” with rounded lips (as in tu) · r = soft gargle at the back of the throat
eh = “e” in bed · ay = “ay” in day, but shorter
